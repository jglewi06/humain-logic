# The best AI for writing your resume

Job hunting is stressful, and AI can take some of the grind out of it. It can turn a messy work history into clear bullet points, tailor your resume to a job post, and suggest stronger wording. It can also invent achievements or sound generic, so every line needs to be true and sound like you.

## Picks for different needs
- **Free and flexible:** ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini on their free plans. Paste a job post and your resume and ask for tailored bullet points.
- **Resume builders:** [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com/pricing) has a free plan plus a job tracker. [Rezi](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing) focuses on applicant tracking systems, with a free plan. [Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com/en/pricing/) has a free basic version.
- **Matching a job post:** [Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/pricing) gives 5 free scans a month.
- **Interview practice:** [Yoodli](https://yoodli.ai/pricing) has a free Starter plan.

Prices checked October 15, 2026.

## Keep it honest
Never let AI add skills or results you don't have. Recruiters can tell, and it can cost you the job. Use AI for structure and wording; the facts come from you.

## FAQ
**What is the best free AI for resumes?** A free chat assistant plus Teal's free plan covers most needs (as of Oct 2026).
**Can employers tell if AI wrote my resume?** Generic phrasing is easy to spot. Edit so it sounds like you and includes specific results.
**Should I use auto-apply tools?** Be cautious. Mass applications rarely beat a few tailored ones.
