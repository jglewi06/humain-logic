# Free AI tools worth trying

You don't need to spend money to get real value from AI. Many of the best tools have free plans that cover everyday use. This roundup lists the ones we'd point a friend to, grouped by what you want to do, with a plain note on what the free plan actually includes.

Free plans change often. Everything below was checked against each tool's own pricing page on **October 15, 2026**. Our [AI tool directory](/tools) has the full, up-to-date list.

## What "free" usually means

"Free" comes in a few flavors, and it helps to know which one you're getting:

- **Free plan:** you can keep using it, with limits (messages per day, credits, watermarks).
- **Free credits:** a one-time or daily allowance; once it's gone, you pay or wait.
- **Free trial:** full features for a few days, then it charges, often to a card you entered. Set a reminder.

We've only included tools with a genuine free plan or free allowance below.

## How to stay safe with free tools

Free tools still have a cost: usually your attention, your data, or both. A few habits help:

- **Read the privacy settings.** Many tools let you turn off chat history or training on your data.
- **Don't paste sensitive information,** like passwords, bank details, medical records or other people's private data.
- **Check the answers.** Free or paid, AI can be confidently wrong.
- **Watch for "free" trials** that need a card, and cancel before they renew if you don't want them.
- **Download apps only from official sites or app stores.** Scammers make fake AI apps.

## FAQ

**What is the best free AI tool?**
For most people, a free chat assistant (ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) is the most useful single tool. Add a specialist tool for images, video or research as needed.

**Are free AI tools really free?**
Many have genuine free plans with limits. Some "free" offers are trials that charge later, so read the pricing page before entering a card.

**Is there a free AI image generator with no sign-up?**
Most require an account. Microsoft Designer and Adobe Firefly's free daily generations need a free Microsoft or Adobe account.

**Can I use free AI tools for my business?**
Often yes, but some free plans (Suno's, for example) exclude commercial use. Check each tool's terms.

**How do you choose which tools to list?**
We list tools with a real free plan, verified on the official pricing page, and note the date we checked.
