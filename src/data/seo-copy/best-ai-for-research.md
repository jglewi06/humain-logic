# The best AI for research

For research, the most important feature is simple: **can you see where the answer came from?** The best AI research tools link to their sources so you can check them. Even then, AI can misread a source, so open the links that matter.

## Picks for different needs
- **Everyday questions with sources:** [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/pro) has a free plan and shows citations.
- **Your own documents:** [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/plans) is free with a Google account and answers questions grounded in the files you upload.
- **Academic papers:** [Elicit](https://elicit.com/pricing) and [Consensus](https://consensus.app/pricing/) both have free plans. [ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai/pricing) maps related papers, free. [SciSpace](https://scispace.com/pricing) has a free tier.
- **Private, ad-free search:** [Kagi](https://kagi.com/pricing) starts at $5/month.

Prices checked October 15, 2026.

## A simple routine
Ask the question, open two or three of the cited sources, and confirm the key facts yourself. If a tool can't show a source, treat the answer as a lead, not a fact.

## FAQ
**What is the best free AI for research?** Perplexity and NotebookLM are strong free starts (as of Oct 2026).
**Which AI is best for academic papers?** Elicit, Consensus, ResearchRabbit and SciSpace are built for papers.
**Can AI make up sources?** Yes. Always open citations to check they exist and say what the AI claims.
**Is AI research good enough for school?** It's a starting point. Follow your school's rules and cite the original sources.
