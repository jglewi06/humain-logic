# The best AI for video: tools to make and edit clips

AI video tools fall into two groups: ones that **create** clips from a prompt or photo, and ones that **edit** your own footage faster, with auto-captions, cutting and clean audio. For most people and small businesses, the editing tools give the most value.

## Picks for different needs
- **Easy editing:** [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/pricing) and Microsoft's [Clipchamp](https://clipchamp.com/en/pricing/) both have free plans. [Descript](https://www.descript.com/pricing) lets you edit video by editing the transcript, with a free plan.
- **Short clips from long videos:** [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/pricing) has a free plan with 60 credits/month.
- **Generate video from text or images:** [Runway](https://runwayml.com/pricing) offers 125 one-time free credits; [Pika](https://pika.art/pricing) has paid plans from $10/month.
- **Talking-head avatars:** [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/pricing) (free Basic, watermarked) and [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/pricing) (free plan, 3 videos/month).

Prices checked October 15, 2026.

## Use it kindly
Never make a video of a real person saying something they didn't say without permission. Label AI-generated video when viewers might be misled.

## FAQ
**What is the best free AI video editor?** CapCut, Clipchamp and Descript all have free plans (as of Oct 2026).
**Can AI make a video from text?** Yes, tools like Runway and Pika generate short clips; quality and length vary.
**Are AI avatar videos good for business?** They're useful for training and explainers; some viewers find them less personal.
