# Will AI replace nurses?

## The short answer
No, and the research points the other way. In our job tracker, registered nurses are rated **"steady,"** and hiring is expected to **grow by 2030**. Hands-on patient care, clinical judgment and compassion make nursing one of the least automatable jobs. The World Economic Forum expects care jobs to be among the biggest growers by 2030 ([WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/)), and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects registered nurse employment to grow 6% from 2025 to 2035, with about 180,800 openings a year ([BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/registered-nurses.htm)).

## How AI is showing up in nursing
AI is arriving in hospitals mostly as a helper: tools that draft documentation and early-warning systems that flag patients who may be getting worse. The hope is less time on charting and more time with patients. These tools have limits and can be wrong, so nurses' judgment, and feedback when a tool misfires, matter a great deal.

## Our estimate and where it comes from
Our automation timeline for registered nurses is **2055 or later, with low confidence**. It's a HumAIn Logic editorial estimate, not a figure from the sources we cite. Long-range forecasts are uncertain, and we say so. See our [methodology](/methodology).

## Next steps for nurses
- Learn the AI tools in your workplace and understand where they fall short.
- Take a healthcare AI course, such as [Stanford's AI in Healthcare on Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/ai-healthcare).
- Advance your specialty; the [American Nurses Association](https://www.nursingworld.org/) has resources on certifications.
- Explore nursing informatics, where nurses bridge clinical care and technology.
- Speak up about safe AI use. Your feedback shapes how these tools are used.

## FAQ
**Will AI replace nurses?** No. Nursing is rated "steady" with expected growth; AI mainly helps with documentation.
**What will AI do in nursing?** Draft notes, flag risks and handle some admin, with nurses checking the results.
**Is nursing a safe career choice in the age of AI?** It's one of the most resilient jobs in our tracker.
**What is nursing informatics?** A specialty where nurses help design and run the technology used in care.
