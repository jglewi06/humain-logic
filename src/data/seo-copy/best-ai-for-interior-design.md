# The best AI for interior design

Want to see your living room in a different style before you buy a single cushion? AI interior design tools take a photo of your room and show it redesigned, from Scandinavian to mid-century. They're great for ideas, less great for exact measurements, and the furniture they show may not exist.

## Picks for different needs
- **Restyle a room from a photo:** [Interior AI](https://interiorai.com/) is advertised at $390/year for Pro. [ReimagineHome](https://www.reimaginehome.ai/pricing) has a free tier, with paid plans from $19/month.
- **Free ideas:** chat assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini can suggest color palettes, layouts and shopping lists, and some can generate or edit images on their free plans.
- **Mood boards:** [Canva](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) has a free plan with AI tools for quick boards.

Prices checked October 15, 2026.

## Before you buy anything
Treat AI images as inspiration. Measure your space, check real product dimensions, and confirm that what you see actually exists in a store.

## FAQ
**Is there a free AI for interior design?** ReimagineHome has a free tier, and free chat assistants can suggest layouts and palettes (as of Oct 2026).
**Can AI design a room from a photo?** Yes, tools like Interior AI restyle a photo, but the results are visual ideas, not plans.
**Are AI room designs accurate?** Not for measurements. Always measure and check real products.
**Can real estate agents use these tools?** Some use them for virtual staging; check local rules about disclosing edited photos.
