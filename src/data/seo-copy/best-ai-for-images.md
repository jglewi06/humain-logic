# The best AI image generators

AI image generators turn a short description into a picture. They're handy for social posts, invitations, mood boards and simple product shots. Results vary a lot by tool, and some still struggle with hands or text in images.

## Picks for different needs
- **Easiest free start:** [Microsoft Designer](https://designer.microsoft.com/) is free with a Microsoft account. [Adobe Firefly](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/plans.html) offers free daily generations, with plans from $9.99/month.
- **Artistic quality:** [Midjourney](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans) has no free plan; Basic is $10/month.
- **Text inside images:** [Ideogram](https://ideogram.ai/pricing) is known for readable text, with free weekly credits for eligible accounts.
- **Lots of styles:** [Leonardo](https://leonardo.ai/pricing) has a free daily allowance; [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/pricing) has a free plan too.
- **Editing photos:** [Clipdrop](https://clipdrop.co/pricing) and [remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg/pricing) handle background removal and clean-ups.

Prices and free allowances checked October 15, 2026.

## Good habits
Read each tool's rules on commercial use before selling images. Don't create images of real people without their consent, and label AI images when it matters.

## FAQ
**What is the best free AI image generator?** Microsoft Designer and Adobe Firefly's free daily generations are easy starts (as of Oct 2026).
**Can I use AI images commercially?** Often yes, but it depends on the tool and plan. Check the terms; some free plans exclude commercial use.
**Which AI is best for text in images?** Ideogram is specifically known for it.
