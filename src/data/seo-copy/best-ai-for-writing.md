# The best AI for writing

AI writing tools can help you start a blank page, tidy up grammar, or rephrase a clumsy sentence. They're best as a helpful editor or brainstorming partner, not a replacement for your own voice. They can also state wrong facts confidently, so check anything that matters.

## Picks for different needs
- **All-round drafting:** [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [Claude](https://claude.com/pricing) and [Gemini](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/) all have free plans and handle emails, letters and outlines well.
- **Grammar and tone:** [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) has a free plan; Pro is $12/month. [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/premium) is good for multiple languages.
- **Rewording:** [QuillBot](https://quillbot.com/premium) and [Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com/plans) both have free plans.
- **Fiction:** [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com/pricing) is built for novelists, with a free trial and plans from $10/month.
- **Clear, simple style:** [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) highlights hard-to-read sentences, free on the web.

Prices checked October 15, 2026.

## A tip that helps every tool
Tell the AI who the reader is, what you want, and your tone ("warm, short, no jargon"). Then edit the result so it sounds like you.

## FAQ
**What is the best free AI for writing?** ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all have free plans for drafting; Grammarly's free plan is good for grammar.
**Is using AI to write cheating?** It depends on the rules where you are. Schools and employers set their own policies, so check first.
**Can AI write in my voice?** It can get closer if you share samples, but editing it yourself is still the best way.
**Will AI replace writers?** Our tracker rates writers and copywriters "watch." See the [writer outlook](/tracker/jobs/writer-copywriter).
