# Will AI replace accountants?

## The short answer
AI will change accounting a lot, but it's unlikely to replace accountants outright soon. In our job tracker, accountants and auditors are rated **"watch,"** with **medium hiring pressure by 2030**. Many accounting tasks can be automated. Professional judgment, regulation and legal liability slow down any full takeover, because someone still has to sign off and be accountable.

## Where the pressure is
The World Economic Forum lists accountants and auditors among roles expected to decline, but the pressure falls mainly on **routine clerical work rather than senior judgment** ([WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/)). That's why bookkeeping, accounting and payroll clerks sit in our higher-pressure "shift" group, while accountants sit one step safer.

In practice, AI handles more of the data entry, matching and first-draft analysis. That shifts accountants toward advice, interpretation and trust.

## Our estimate and where it comes from
Our automation timeline for accountants and auditors is **2040–2055, with low confidence**. This is a HumAIn Logic editorial estimate, not a number published by the sources we cite, which include the WEF and [Microsoft Research's 2025 study](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/working-with-ai-measuring-the-occupational-implications-of-generative-ai/). See our [methodology](/methodology).

## How accountants can stay ahead
- Build data skills: Excel, Power BI and visualization help you spot insights and anomalies. [Microsoft Learn's Power BI training](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/powerplatform/power-bi/) is free.
- Learn how AI tools test transactions and draft analysis, and how to review their output. [AICPA & CIMA](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/) offers continuing education.
- Move toward advisory work, forecasting and tax planning, which rely on relationships.
- Explore adjacent roles like forensic accountant, IT auditor or controller on [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org/).

## FAQ
**Will AI replace accountants?** Not soon. It's taking over routine tasks, which pushes accountants toward judgment and advice.
**Are bookkeepers more at risk than accountants?** Yes. In our tracker, bookkeeping and payroll clerks face higher pressure.
**Is accounting still a good career?** It's changing, but advisory, audit judgment and tax planning still need people. Pairing accounting with data skills helps.
**What AI skills should accountants learn?** Spreadsheet and data analysis, plus knowing how to check AI-generated work.
